# Source discovery for the FDE journey

The source index in `../../workspace/resources.md` preserves source names, URLs, and stable IDs used by existing journey references.

These sources are optional starting points only. They do not define an exclusive reading list, fixed source hierarchy, prescribed chapter sequence, or limit on research.

For every topic:

- Search all relevant books and documents available in ChatGPT Project Sources, including sources not named in the unit.
- Explore related terminology, mechanisms, examples, failure modes, trade-offs, and cross-references across the full relevant books.
- Use the strongest additional books, primary research, standards, official documentation, and incident reports that improve the explanation.
- Do not rely only on the source IDs listed by the unit.
- Do not track which chapters or pages have been read, and do not use reading-status markers.
- Teach the relevant substance directly; citations identify the material but never replace the explanation.

The learner's mission and journey define what capability to develop. They do not restrict which sources may be used to teach it.
