# Source policy



## Rules

- **SRC-01** Project books MUST be consulted first.
- **SRC-02** External sources MUST be modern and limited to major companies, official documentation, major-vendor engineering blogs, standards bodies, or peer-reviewed work.
- **SRC-03** Forums, SEO blogs, and unvetted content MUST NOT be used.
- **SRC-04** Books win for fundamentals; a newer reputable source wins for fast-changing technology. Always flag disagreement.
- **SRC-05** Cite books with book title plus chapter or section. Cite external sources with URL plus access date.
- **SRC-06** If no qualifying source exists, say so instead of guessing.
