# Evidence



## Supported by evidence

- Empty until a session records a demonstrated capability.

## Needs learning

- Engineering foundations and AI application design.
- Evaluation, regression testing, tracing, security, deployment, adoption, and business measurement.
- Provider integration fault testing, rollback, reconciliation, runtime diagnosis, and runbook creation.

## Must not be claimed

- Production deployment, users, metrics, or ownership without recorded proof.
- Production experience from labs, stubs, rehearsals, or simulated incidents.
- Framework skill from agent-generated code without review and explanation.
- Platform capabilities not designed or implemented by the learner.
