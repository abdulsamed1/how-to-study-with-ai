# Mission: Become a hireable Frontier Deployed Engineer (FDE)

## Why

Get hired as an FDE by showing one real, evaluated, secured, deployed, and handed-over agentic system and defending every design decision.

## Success looks like

- Decide whether AI is appropriate for an ambiguous business problem and justify autonomy.
- Maintain a capstone repository with tests, adversarial evaluation, tracing, security review, deployment, and runbook.
- Defend architecture decisions, threat model, evaluation report, and runbook.
- Diagnose a production incident from project artifacts and state what has and has not been done.

## Constraints

- The learner owns learning progress and current position. At the beginning of a session, the learner names the journey, unit, and part to continue; the mentor does not infer completion from chat history or persist a progress ledger.
- The repository stores the mission, curriculum, teaching/source rules, and actual capstone artifacts — not personal lesson-by-lesson status.
- Explanations follow [governance/language-and-style.md](../governance/language-and-style.md).

## Out of scope

- Foundation-model training, ML research, and pure prompt engineering as an end.
- Demos without a credible path to production.
